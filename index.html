Coorg Sightseeing Tour from Bangalore

Relish the sightseeing tour of Coorg, also known as "Kashmir of the South". Popularly known for its coffee estates and rich flora and fauna, Coorg is a must-visit destination in Karnataka for nature and culture enthusiasts alike.

Trip Highlights

  • Enjoy sightseeing in the popular destination of Karnataka, Coorg
  • Visit Abbey Falls, Madikeri Fort, Dubare Elephant Camp, and more
  • Witness the scenic Raja’s Seat with breathtaking views

Detailed Overview

Coorg, also known as the Scotland of India, is famous for its coffee plantations and lush Western Ghats landscapes. Madikeri, the heart of Coorg, offers misty hills, tea and coffee estates, orange groves, undulating streets, and stunning viewpoints, making it an unforgettable holiday destination.

Itinerary

Pickup from Bangalore:
Driver picks you up from your hotel/residence at the chosen time. Approx. 5-hour drive to Coorg.
Tour Stops:
  • Dubare Elephant Camp – Get close to elephants and create memorable moments
  • Nisargadhama Forest Park – Scenic spot with River Kaveri, dense forests, and birdlife
  • Namdroling Buddhist Golden Temple – One of India’s largest Tibetan settlements
  • Abbey Falls – Scenic waterfall surrounded by coffee and spice plantations
  • Omkareshwara Temple – Built in 1820, features Mohammedan architecture
  • Madikeri Fort – Historic fort with museum, chapel, temple, and prison
  • Government Museum – Sculptures from 11th century AD and Coorg history
  • Gaddige (Raja's Tomb) – Tombs of Virarajendra and Lingarajendra with hilltop views
  • Raja's Seat – Sunset viewpoint of the kings
Return:
After the tour, drop back to hotel/residence in Bangalore.

Basic Details

  • Start & Finish Point: Hotel/Residence, Bangalore
  • Start Time Options: 6:00 AM, 7:00 AM, 8:00 AM, 8:30 AM
  • Categories: Tours & Sightseeing, Family-friendly
  • Trip Code: O885A3652

Inclusions

  • All sightseeing using private AC vehicle
  • Meal for the driver
  • Vehicle & fuel charges
  • Tour as per the itinerary
  • 14 hours usage of vehicle

Exclusions

  • Monument entrance fees or tickets

Hampi Guided Tour

Spiritual Heritage: Mantralayam & Hampi – 1 Night/2 Days Tour from Bengaluru

Embark on a journey of spiritual discovery and historical exploration with our 1 Night/2 Days Mantralayam & Hampi Tour. This itinerary combines a visit to Mantralayam, the sacred abode of Sri Guru Raghavendra Swamy, with the awe-inspiring ruins of Hampi, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Experience the best of Karnataka’s spiritual and architectural heritage.

Itinerary Highlights

Day 1 – Spiritual Mantralayam & Hospet:
08:00 PM – Departure from Bengaluru (Yeshwanthpur BMTC Bus Stand).
05:30 AM – Arrival at Mantralayam for Darshan of Sri Raghavendra Swamy at Brindavana (freshen up).
09:30 AM – Depart for Hospet.
05:00 PM – Arrive at Hotel Mayura Vijayanagara / Similar, TB Dam Hospet for overnight stay.
Day 2 – Hampi's Ruins & Return to Bengaluru:
08:00 AM – 01:30 PM – Hampi Sightseeing: Virupaksha Temple, Kadale Kalu Ganesha, Sasive Kalu Ganesha, Lakshmi Narasimha, Badavi Linga, Lotus Mahal, Elephant Stables, Hazara Rama Temple, Palace Area, Mahanavami Dibba, Queen’s Bath.
01:30 PM – 02:30 PM – Lunch Break at Hotel Mayura Bhuvaneshwari Hampi.
02:30 PM – 04:00 PM – Visit Archaeological Museum to learn more about Hampi’s history.
04:30 PM – 06:00 PM – Explore Vijaya Vithala Temple (Stone Chariot), King’s Balance, Purandara Mantapa, Old Bridge Pillars.
08:15 PM – 09:30 PM – Dinner at Hotel Mayura Vijayanagara, TB Dam.
09:30 PM – Departure to Bengaluru.

Who Can Choose This Package?

  • Pilgrims and spiritual seekers
  • History and archaeology enthusiasts
  • Travelers interested in exploring UNESCO World Heritage Sites
